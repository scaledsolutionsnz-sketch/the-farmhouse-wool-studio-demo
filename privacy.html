Privacy Policy

What the studio does with your details when you ring, email or use this website.

Placeholder wording for the demo. Replace with a policy checked by the studio before the site goes live.

1. The short version

The Farmhouse Wool Studio is a small shop in Marton. Your details are used to answer your question, take your order or set something aside for you. They are not sold, rented or traded, and no marketing list is built from this website.

2. This website collects nothing directly

There is no contact form, no feedback form and no newsletter signup anywhere on this site. Nothing you type is captured by the studio through these pages. The buttons on the site simply open your phone dialler, your email, Google Maps or a social media page.

3. What the studio does hold

  • Your name and contact details when you ring or email about stock, an order or a class.
  • Order and purchase details where goods are ordered in for you, held aside or paid for.
  • Any notes needed to get your project right, such as pattern, quantity or dye lot.

That information is held only for as long as it is needed for the order or enquiry, and for any period required by tax and business record keeping rules.

4. Third party services used on this site

  • Google Maps supplies the embedded map. Loading a map can set cookies and share your IP address with Google under Google's own privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces used on these pages.
  • Google, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok are linked from the site. If you follow a link, that platform's own privacy policy applies from that point.
  • The website host keeps standard server logs, which may include IP addresses, for security and reliability.

5. Payments

Payments are taken in the studio. Card details are handled by the payment terminal provider and are not stored by the studio.

6. Reviews and social media

Reviews you leave on Google, Facebook or another platform are public and controlled by you and that platform, not by the studio. If a photograph of your work is shared on the studio's social media, it will only be with your say so.

7. Sharing your information

Details are shared only where they are needed to fulfil what you have asked for, for example passing an order to a supplier such as Ashford, or where the law requires it.

8. Keeping it safe

Reasonable steps are taken to keep your information secure, including limiting who can see it. No system is perfect, so absolute security cannot be promised.

9. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see the personal information the studio holds about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted where there is no longer a reason to keep it. Ring or email and it will be sorted.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version always sits on this page.

11. Getting in touch

Privacy questions can go to the studio by phone on 020 4152 6282 or by email.
