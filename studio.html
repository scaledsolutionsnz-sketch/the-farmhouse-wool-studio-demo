Inside the studio

A shop the size of a front room, and a long list of suppliers.

Everything below is on the shelves in Marton or can be brought in. Ring before you drive over and Linda will check and set it aside.

Yarn and fibre

Wool worth the trip.

Most of it comes from small South Island outlets, a good deal of it from Canterbury, found in person rather than ordered out of a catalogue.

Yarn

Fine spun and glowing, from single skeins through to jersey quantities. Sock yarns, naturals, hand dyed one offs and the odd bag that will not be repeated.

Fibre and fleece

Tops, batts and raw fleece for spinning and felting. Bring your hands. Fibre is very hard to judge from a photograph.

Ashford wheels and looms

As the local Ashford agent, the studio carries wheels, rigid heddle and table looms, carders, bobbins, drive bands and the spares that stop a job dead.

The small stuff

Needles, buttons and the things that finish a piece.

The bits people always run out of halfway through, plus a few they did not know they wanted.

Needles, hooks and notions

Straights, circulars, interchangeables, hooks, stitch markers, row counters and project bags. Plus shelves and small boxes of ceramic, porcelain and plastic buttons.

Patterns and help

Jerseys, toys and the clever two needle socks worked out by a local designer. Bring the tangle with you and we will find the dropped stitch together.

Gifts and handmade finds

Knitted rabbits with real personality made by a local woman, and bespoke leather bags from South Island makers. Good for the knitter who has everything.

A look around

Have a wander before you drive over.
